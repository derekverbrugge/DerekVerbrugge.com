Why I'm asking for help

In late August, I was attacked by a dog, and it injured my back badly enough that I needed MRIs for back spasms. Those scans showed multiple compressed disks, and the injury put me out of work.

I had jobs lined up, but because of my work restrictions, I couldn't take them.

Just as I was trying to return to work, my car suffered a differential/transmission failure and is effectively totaled. Before my car failed, I already had a temporary job and a part-time job lined up.

Right now, this has left me in a very hard position. I'm unable to find work, and I can't pay my rent or my bills. Losing work after the attack was already a major setback, and without a working car, it's been even harder to prepare for and accept the work offers I am getting.

Every day this goes on, it gets harder to keep up and harder to move forward.

Working toward a way forward

I'm hoping to receive backpay related to the attack, and I have a lawyer working on it, but that process is moving slowly. I have also applied for Vocational Rehabilitation to get assistance taking the work offers I already have available, but I need help now while those steps are still pending.

I'm asking for help so I can cover my rent and get through my immediate bills while I work on getting back on my feet. My plan is to use this support to stay current on rent and handle the immediate pressure long enough to take the work offers I'm getting but can't prepare for right now because of my situation.

Please consider helping

I need help as soon as possible. If you're able to donate, any amount would help me keep a roof over my head and give me a real chance to take the work that's in front of me.

If you can't donate, sharing this fundraiser would mean a lot to me and could help me reach someone who can.

Thank you for your support.
